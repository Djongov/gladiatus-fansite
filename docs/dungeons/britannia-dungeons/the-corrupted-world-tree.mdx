The Corrupted World Tree is the second of the new Britannia Dungeons.

<DungeonFacts slug="britannia-dungeons/the-corrupted-world-tree" />

Level on which you can complete: **135**

## Description

In the midst of an enchanted forest, you acome across a huge, corrupted tree. The clearing is filled with glowing red lights, making the corruption almost palpable. Investigate the secrets of this grove before the forces of darkness take dominion over you as well.

## Evil Fairy Queen Clíodhna (boss)

<DungeonEnemy slug="britannia-dungeons/the-corrupted-world-tree" enemy="evil-fairy-queen-cliodhna" />

### Special abilities

She never attacks in a round when she uses an ability, and she uses at most one per round. When two are due together, the later one waits a round.

**Round 1, 6, 11, 16 or every 5 rounds: A hazy shimmer surrounds Evil Fairy Queen Clíodhna, as she is protected by a shield of pure energy, causing every blow to bounce off harmlessly in a shower of sparks.**

The shield absorbs 3,000 damage and lasts until it breaks or 4 rounds pass. A blow that breaks it carries the rest of its damage through. In a 49-round fight her shields absorbed 25,981 damage, more than her whole life.

**Round 2, 14, 27, 39 or every 12 rounds: Evil Fairy Queen Clíodhna pounds the ground with an almighty thud, sending cracks through the earth, inflicting a paralysing weakness over her enemies that saps even powers of healing.**

No effect has been seen yet; sometimes the game says the pounding has no effect at all.

**Round 3, 15, 28, 40 or every 12 rounds: Seized by a raging fury, Evil Fairy Queen Clíodhna lets loose a deafening roar, and her attacks strike down with twice the force, leaving nothing but devastation in their wake.**

Her next attack does double damage. A miss uses it up.
