Before the Dawn is the fifth of the new Britannia Dungeons.

<DungeonFacts slug="britannia-dungeons/before-the-dawn" />

Level on which you can complete: **180**

## Description

Troops of Britons attacked the Roman coastal encampment in a night-time raid. Now that the troops outside the walls have been defeated, it is time for the siege to begin!

![Keeper of the Ritual](https://gladiatusfansite.blob.core.windows.net/images/Dungeons/Britannia/before-the-dawn/before-the-dawn.jpg)

## Quests

1. Defend against the attackers
2. Fight back the remaining troops
3. Defeat the leader
